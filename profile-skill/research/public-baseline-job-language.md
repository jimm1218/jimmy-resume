## Role Language

### Business Intelligence / BI Analyst

O*NET describes BI Analysts as producing business, financial, or market intelligence by querying data repositories and generating periodic reports, plus identifying data patterns and trends. O*NET task language emphasizes:

- Generate standard or custom reports for executives, managers, clients, and stakeholders.
- Maintain or update BI tools, databases, dashboards, systems, or methods.
- Manage timely flow of BI information to users.
- Document specifications for reports, dashboards, and outputs.
- Create BI tools or systems, including related databases, spreadsheets, or outputs.
- Synthesize trend data to support recommendations for action.

Resume implication:

- Lead BI experience with `Power BI dashboard`, `KPI definition`, `DAX`, `Power Query`, `SQL`, `stakeholder reporting`, `business decision support`.
- Keep the language close to business outcomes, not only tool lists.

### Power BI Data Analyst

Microsoft PL-300 frames the Power BI data analyst as someone who delivers actionable insights by working with available data and domain expertise, enables self-service analytics, and works with stakeholders, analytics engineers, and data engineers. Skills measured as of 2026 include:

- Prepare data.
- Model data.
- Visualize and analyze data.
- Manage and secure Power BI.

Detailed language useful for resumes:

- Identify and connect to data sources.
- Profile, clean, transform, and load data.
- Create fact tables and dimension tables.
- Create relationships, date tables, calculated columns, calculated tables, and DAX measures.
- Select appropriate visuals, configure reports, apply filters, customize themes, enable storytelling, and identify trends, outliers, and anomalies.
- Configure workspaces, refresh, access, and row-level security.

Resume implication:

- For BI direction, `Power BI`, `DAX`, `Power Query M`, `data modeling`, `KPI`, `semantic model`, `refresh`, `governance`, and `self-service analytics` are strong terms.

### Data Analyst

Google and 104 sources converge on data analyst language around using data to solve business problems, cleaning and analyzing data, visualization/storytelling, and tools such as SQL, spreadsheets, Tableau/Power BI, Python/R.

Useful resume language:

- Data cleaning and transformation.
- SQL querying and data extraction.
- Dashboard/report development.
- Business problem framing.
- Visualization and data storytelling.
- Insight generation for decisions.
- Cross-functional communication.

104 Taiwan search results repeatedly surface:

- Power BI dashboard/report design, development, and maintenance.
- SQL query and data processing.
- ETL and data integration.
- Data model / KPI indicator system.
- Cross-functional communication with business units.
- Improving reporting processes for accuracy, reliability, timeliness, and usability.

Resume implication:

- The candidate's strongest match is not entry-level generic `data analyst`; it is business-facing analytics with BI, CRM/CDP, SQL, and dashboard ownership.

### AI Application Data Analyst

104's AI talent article positions data analysts between AI application and AI development: translating data into actionable insights, supporting AI adoption effect evaluation, business optimization, data cleaning, EDA, visualization reports, KPI tracking, insight reports, and cross-functional requirement communication. It lists SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, statistics, Python/R, visualization, problem decomposition, and business logic.

104 AI/Data Python job language adds:

- Data acquisition and crawling from social/public platforms.
- ETL flow design, cleaning, transformation, integration, and standardization.
- Scheduled automation.
- Databases such as MySQL/PostgreSQL/MongoDB/Elasticsearch.
- Backend/API development with Django, Flask, or FastAPI.
- API documentation and frontend collaboration.
- Git, Linux, Docker.
- AI/data application support and model output formats.

Resume implication:

- GROUP-PROJECT should be framed as an AI application data product, not as full-time AI engineer experience.
- Strong terms: `FastAPI`, `Supabase`, `ML risk classification`, `LLM API`, `RAG guardrails`, `Dashboard`, `data writeback`, `Docker`, `GCP VM`, `GitHub Actions`, `AI-assisted response workflow`.

## Candidate Fit

The candidate's public baseline should not be a pure `Data Scientist` resume. The best fit is:

> BI / Data Analyst with CRM/CDP and SQL depth, plus AI application project experience.

Why:

- FILA and Wang Steak match BI analyst language: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, CRM/CDP, KPI, stakeholder reporting, data preparation.
- ETMall and 向上國際 match automation, SQL, stored procedures, reporting pipeline, and operational analysis.
- GROUP-PROJECT matches AI application analyst language: FastAPI, Supabase, ML risk classification, LLM API, dashboard, data writeback, Docker, GCP VM.
- The current source evidence supports `8+ years analytics / market research / CRM-CDP / SQL-BI`, but not `8+ years AI development`.

## Recommended Public Positioning Options

### Option A: BI-First

Headline:

> BI 分析師 / 資料分析師 / AI 應用資料分析

Positioning:

> 具 8 年以上商業數據分析、CRM/CDP、SQL 與 Power BI 經驗，擅長將會員經營、行銷活動與營運問題轉化為 KPI、資料模型與管理儀表板，並以 AI 應用專案延伸至可部署資料產品。

Best when:

- Applying for BI Analyst, Power BI Analyst, CRM Analyst, Data Analyst roles.

Tradeoff:

- AI project becomes a strong differentiator, not the headline.

### Option B: AI Application-First

Headline:

> AI 應用資料分析 / BI 分析師 / 資料分析師

Positioning:

> 具商業數據分析、BI 與 CRM/CDP 經驗的 AI 應用資料分析師，能將營運問題拆解為資料流程、模型判斷、儀表板與可部署應用，近期以 GROUP-PROJECT 完成 FastAPI、Supabase、ML、LLM API、Docker 與 GCP VM 整合專題。

Best when:

- Applying for AI Application Analyst, AI Data Analyst, AI/BI engineer-adjacent roles.

Tradeoff:

- May over-index on recent project work if the employer is seeking production AI engineering depth.

### Option C: General Data Analyst

Headline:

> 資料分析師 / BI 分析師 / 數據分析師

Positioning:

> 具 8 年以上數據分析、市場研究、CRM/CDP、SQL 與儀表板建置經驗，能將跨部門需求轉化為資料處理流程、分析報告、KPI 追蹤與決策建議，並具 Python 與 AI 應用專案實作經驗。

Best when:

- No target job exists and the resume needs broad coverage.

Tradeoff:

- Less distinctive than BI-first or AI-application-first.

## Recommended Baseline Choice

Recommended: Option A, BI-first.

Reason:

- It is most strongly supported by career evidence.
- It aligns with Taiwan 104 job language around Power BI, SQL, ETL, KPI, dashboards, stakeholder communication, and report maintenance.
- It still leaves room to feature GROUP-PROJECT as a modern AI application differentiator.

## Risks To Avoid

- Do not imply 8+ years of AI engineering.
- Do not list private repo as if reviewers can access it.
- Do not let secondary practice projects dilute GROUP-PROJECT.
- Do not lead with `Data Scientist` unless a target JD explicitly asks for ML/statistical modeling.
- Do not overstate cloud/platform ownership beyond confirmed GCP VM, Docker, Nginx HTTPS, GitHub Actions, and daily sync evidence.
